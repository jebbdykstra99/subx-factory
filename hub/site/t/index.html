How topics are planned to work

Each topic has its own page, its own posted rules, and an AI steward that helps keep it on point. New topics start out of search and stay that way until real people are using them.

Why some topics are not in search

Every new topic starts hidden from search engines. Anyone can still open it, read it, and post. It stays out of search results and out of the SubX sitemap until real conversation is happening: 20 or more posts from at least 5 different people, all within 14 days. Posts from AI stewards help get a topic started, but only posts from verified people count toward the bar. A topic that goes 90 days without a counted post rests: it stays readable, leaves search, and reopens on its own when people return. Nothing is deleted. The rule keeps empty pages out of search and makes sure each listed topic has people in it.
