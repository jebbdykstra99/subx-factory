How we handle reports: remove first

When a report comes in, we may hide the reported content right away while we review it. A person decides whether hidden content is restored, and makes the final decision before we suspend an account.

We may hide first, then review, for these reports:

  • An intimate image of a person shared without their consent.
  • A copyright notice that appears valid. See our copyright policy.
  • A violent, gory or offensive image.
  • A hateful or racist image.

Choose the matching reason when you tap Report. Our community guidelines list what isn’t allowed.

TAKE IT DOWN: an intimate image or video of me was posted without my consent

Under the federal TAKE IT DOWN Act, you, or someone authorized to act for you, can ask us to remove an intimate image or video of you that was posted without your consent. This includes real photos and videos and realistic AI-made or edited images. A valid request has 4 parts:

  1. Your signature. A physical or electronic signature from you, or from someone authorized to act for you. Typing your full name works as an electronic signature.
  2. Where the image is. Enough information for us to identify and find it, for example the link to each post or story.
  3. A short good-faith statement. Say that you believe in good faith that the image is an intimate visual depiction of you that was published without your consent, and include any information that helps us verify that.
  4. How to reach you. Your contact information, such as an email address.

Please don’t send us a copy of the image.

Send your request to: takedown@subx.it (subject “NCII removal request: SubX”).

We review reports and act on them as quickly as we can. If you report an intimate image of you shared without your consent, we will remove it, and any identical copies we know about, within 48 hours of receiving a valid request.

In the app, you can also tap Report on the post or story and choose the intimate-image option. Please still email the 4 parts above so we have a valid request.

Content that may involve a child

Use the Report link on the post in its room, or email takedown@subx.it. Give us the link only. Don’t download, screenshot, or send the image. We hide it immediately, preserve it as federal law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). You can also report directly to NCMEC’s CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

Submitting a request you know is false may violate our Terms and the law.
