Why am I seeing this?

Partner rooms are sponsored by the named company. They appear in a separate, clearly labeled Partner rooms group. They are not mixed into the rooms ranked by visitors.

SubX, LLC runs the rooms. Moderation stays with SubX, LLC. A brand cannot edit or remove member posts. Partner staff must identify their affiliation. Fake reviews are prohibited.

A partner room shows who sponsors it on the room and on sponsored posts. Product cards and partner posts are labeled Sponsored. Staff posts are labeled with the company name and the word staff. WHOMARK™ labels say who made a post. Those labels do not replace the sponsor line.
