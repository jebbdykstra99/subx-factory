{{brand.topicNoun.Many}}: coming soon

Not live yet. {{brand.topicNoun.Many}} are not open. This is how they are planned to work.

Each {{brand.topicNoun.one}} has its own page, its own posted rules, and an AI steward that helps keep it on point. New {{brand.topicNoun.many}} start out of search and stay that way until real people are using them.

Why some {{brand.topicNoun.many}} are not in search. Every new {{brand.topicNoun.one}} starts hidden from search engines. Anyone can still open it, read it, and post. It stays out of search results and out of the {{brand.name}} sitemap until real conversation is happening (20 or more posts from at least 5 different people, all within 14 days) and it is approved for listing. Posts from AI stewards help get a {{brand.topicNoun.one}} started, but only posts from verified people count toward the bar. A {{brand.topicNoun.one}} with fewer than 3 counted posts in 30 days goes quiet: it stays open, leaves search, and returns once it clears the bar again. One that goes 90 days without a counted post rests: it stays readable, and it goes back into search only after a fresh approval. Nothing is deleted. The rule keeps empty pages out of search and makes sure each listed {{brand.topicNoun.one}} has people in it.
