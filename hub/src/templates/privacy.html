2. Information we collect

  • Account data when you register: email address, password (our sign-in provider stores it only as a hash), display name / handle, and a site identifier (siteId). Your public handle is something you choose. We do not make it from your email address, and we do not show your email address to other users.
  • Sign-in providers: if Google (or another provider) is enabled, we receive identifiers and profile fields the provider shares with us when you authorize sign-in.
  • Hub accounts: signing in on the hub uses the same {{brand.name}} account as the rooms. When you sign in on the hub, we store your account ID (uid), your display name, the hub’s site identifier (siteId), and the basic profile fields your sign-in provider shares with us (for Google: your name, email address, and profile photo link). Follows and direct messages you make with that account in a room are stored with your account. Follows you make on the hub stay in your browser only (see Hub follows below). The hub does not take posts, replies, or stories.
  • Content you submit: posts, replies, images, polls, direct messages (where enabled), reports, and similar UGC.
  • Usage / device data: approximate logs such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, timestamps, and App Check / abuse-prevention signals needed to secure Firebase Auth, Firestore, and Storage. For abuse prevention, App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA, which runs on the pages where you can sign in and sends browser and device signals to Google to tell people from automated traffic. Request logs kept in {{brand.legalEntity}}'s own Google Cloud project (such as those for the visit beacon and link previews) are deleted automatically after about 30 days. The pages are hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google), and sign-in, database and file storage run on Google Firebase; those providers keep their own server logs, including IP addresses, under their own retention policies, which {{brand.legalEntity}} does not control.
  • Automated-traffic signals: {{brand.legalEntity}}'s visit records do not store your browser's user agent, brand headers, language settings, or automation (headless or webdriver) flags. Google Cloud's standard request logs for the visit beacon record the IP address, browser user agent and referring page along with the visitor ID, and are kept for about 30 days. They may be reviewed to detect abuse or automated traffic.
  • Unique-visitor ID: to count unique visitors, {{brand.legalEntity}} has each browser create a random visitor ID that does not contain your name or contact details, kept in the browser's local storage as subx.vid, and sends it with the visit beacon. {{brand.legalEntity}}'s visit records do not include a name, email address, account ID, or IP address. Google Cloud's standard request logs for the visit beacon record the IP address, browser user agent and referring page along with the visitor ID, and are kept for about 30 days. Individual visit records are deleted after about 90 days, but {{brand.legalEntity}} keeps a record of each visitor ID seen on {{brand.name}} and the dates it was first and last seen for up to 25 months after it was last seen, so long-term unique-visitor counts stay accurate. Clearing the browser's site data creates a new ID. To ask {{brand.legalEntity}} to delete a visitor ID, email {{brand.takedownEmail}} with the subject “Delete my visitor ID: {{brand.name}}” and, if you can, the subx.vid value from your browser. {{brand.legalEntity}} does not use the visitor ID for advertising or cross-site tracking, does not combine it with other data to identify you, and does not sell or share it. If you are in the EU, UK, or Switzerland, {{brand.legalEntity}} keeps this record on the basis of its legitimate interest in accurate audience counts, and you may object or request deletion at the address above.
  • Cookies / local storage: session and preference data required to keep you signed in and remember UI settings. We do not currently run third-party advertising pixels on the Service; if that changes, we will update this Policy.

Hub follows: when you follow rooms on {{brand.primaryDomain}}, the list of rooms you follow and a list of the highlights you have already seen are kept only in your browser's local storage as hub.follows.v1. They are not sent to {{brand.legalEntity}} or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them. You can also unfollow rooms at any time.

Backend services for the Service currently include Google Firebase on project subx-skins (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check). Google’s processing is described in Google’s cloud / Firebase terms and privacy documentation.
